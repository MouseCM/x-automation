Deviantly — Product Report
Oct 15, 2026 · @Deviant
Overview
Deviantly (app.deviantly.ai) is an e-commerce intelligence platform for TikTok Shop sellers, creators and brands. It ranks what is selling right now — videos, livestreams, shops, brands and hashtags — by estimated GMV, then helps users turn those signals into products, scripts and media.
Its positioning: spotting a trend is easy; knowing which one is worth acting on before everyone else is the hard part. The product answers that with GMV-ranked leaderboards rather than raw view counts, plus AI tools to act on what it finds.
The default view is TikTok Shop, United States, with filters for region, date range, category, GMV, units sold and viewers.

Strengths, gaps and recommendations
Deviantly's edge is covering the whole find → validate → create loop with GMV as the common currency; the gaps are mostly about explaining why something ranks.
Strengths
•	Money-first ranking (GMV, units, revenue) instead of vanity metrics, which matches the "worth acting on" positioning.
•	Breadth: livestreams, shops, brands, hashtags and cross-platform video in one place, with deep filters.
•	Built-in action layer — Viral Hooks, Viral Ideas and Media Studio turn a signal into content without leaving the app.
•	Freshness is visible: "Updated 2 min ago", a 10-second sync timer and live counters build trust.
Gaps observed
•	Leaderboard rank does not follow GMV order (#2 shows $9.5K, #3 shows $13.47K) and the ranking rule is not explained.
•	Many rows show "No title" or "-" for length (carousels), making rows hard to scan.
•	No product name or category on leaderboard rows — users must click View to learn what is being sold.
•	After switching pages, the Media Studio dropdown stayed open over the content.
Recommendations
1.	Show the ranking formula (or a "why it ranks" tooltip) and add sorting by GMV.
2.	Add product name, category and price columns to the leaderboards.
3.	Add a GMV-per-1K-followers column — the snapshot shows small accounts dominate, and this ratio surfaces repeatable formats.
4.	Flag repeat winners (creators or products appearing several times) as a "proven format" badge.
5.	Fill missing titles from captions or the linked product, and label carousels clearly.
