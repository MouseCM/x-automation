## What it is
Deviantly.ai is an AI-powered e-commerce intelligence platform. Tagline: "Let AI find your next bestseller."
It scans products, prices, competitors and creators across marketplaces 24/7: Amazon, Shopee, Lazada,
TikTok Shop, Tiki, Walmart, eBay, Etsy, Shopify stores and more.

## Who it's for
Sellers and brands, creators and affiliates, agencies, data teams and developers.

## Products
- Shop Intelligence: revenue, growth, items sold and best sellers for any store, for competitor benchmarking.
- Product Research: sales velocity, pricing and seller counts, with alerts for breakouts and saturation.
- Creator Intelligence: creators ranked by attributed GMV, with content performance and product affinity.
- Marketing Intelligence: search demand, promotion and price data by channel, plus saturation warnings.
- Deviantly Trends: video trend tracking for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- Deviantly API: 28 APIs covering Amazon, TikTok Shop, Instagram and YouTube.

## Offer
7-day free trial on every product. Trends Pro $39/month, Track Seller $79/month, All-in $99/month.

## Voice
- Sharp, curious, data-minded operator. Talks like a founder who lives in marketplace data, not like an ad.
- Lead with the insight from the post, then the Deviantly angle. Value first, product second.
- Plain English, short sentences, concrete nouns. No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changer").
- At most one hashtag, at most one emoji, often none.
- Refer to the product as "deviantly.ai" or "Deviantly".
